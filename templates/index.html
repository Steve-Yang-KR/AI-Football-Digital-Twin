Why this is a Digital Twin

The physical match and this digital model remain connected. New observations update positions and metrics. The model can then be used for replay, prediction and what-if simulation.

Reality → Capture → Digital State

The first working product goal is not a global scouting network. It is a reliable pipeline that converts 2–3 smartphone views into one synchronized match state.

1. QR Pairing

Connect LEFT, CENTER and RIGHT phones.

2. Video Capture

Record the same match from overlapping views.

3. Synchronization

Align frames using time and visual events.

4. Calibration

Map camera pixels to football-field coordinates.

5. AI Tracking

Detect players, ball and events.

6. Twin Update

Update the digital match state continuously.

Player Twin

A persistent model of one player: technical actions, physical output, tactical behavior, development and risk.

Match Twin

A time-based model of positions, possession, pressure, space, events and tactical context.

Team Twin

A model of formation, spacing, compatibility, workload and collective behavior.

The product in one sentence

Use ordinary smartphones to observe a football match, maintain a live digital model of players, ball and team behavior, and simulate decisions before coaches act in the real world.

1. Physical world

The real training session or match.

2. Observation

Smartphones, video and later wearables.

3. Digital Twin

A synchronized, structured state—not just stored video.

4. Simulation

Test formation, pressing, substitutions and workload.

5. Decision

Help coaches and players choose the next action.
