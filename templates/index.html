    Trust is part of the Digital Twin architecture

    Youth safety, consent, evidence quality and human validation determine what the twin can do.

    01Guardian first

    Under-13 profiles, visibility and opportunity requests remain guardian-controlled.

    02Verified adults

    Coaches, scouts, traders and club users pass identity and credential workflows.

    03Permissioned evidence

    Only explicitly approved video, metrics and passport data are shared.

    04Human validation

    AI observations remain provisional until an authorized professional reviews them.
