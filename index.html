Build three connected Digital Twins

The purpose is not simply to analyze one video. It is to maintain continuously updated models of the player, match and team.

🧍

Player Digital Twin

Technical, tactical, physical, cognitive and injury-risk information becomes a growing football identity and career graph.

🏟️

Match Digital Twin

Player and ball positions, events, formations, pressing and space occupation are reconstructed over time.

🧠

Team Digital Twin

Line-up compatibility, formation choices, substitutions, workloads and tactical alternatives are compared.
