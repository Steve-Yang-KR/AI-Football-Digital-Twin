European Digital Twin reference framework

The football architecture is inspired by recurring European Digital Twin principles. These references are conceptual benchmarks, not claimed project partnerships.

EU SYSTEM-SCALE MODEL

Destination Earth

Benchmark principle: connect real-world observations to continuously updated twins, scenario simulation and decision support.

FIELD VALIDATION MODEL

LiDO / Laimburg

Benchmark principle: instrument a physical environment, compare scenarios, validate recommendations and transfer knowledge to users.

RESEARCH & AI MODEL

Wageningen University

Benchmark principle: integrate sensors, computer vision, robotics, models and controlled validation in a living research system.

EARTH OBSERVATION MODEL

ESA Digital Twin Earth

Benchmark principle: fuse distributed data sources and AI to reconstruct changing real-world systems.

INDUSTRY 4.0 MODEL

Fraunhofer

Benchmark principle: link physical assets, operations, prediction, quality and optimization in an interoperable twin.

INDUSTRIAL PLATFORM MODEL

Siemens

Benchmark principle: move from physical asset to digital representation, simulation and lifecycle optimization.
